text-to-speech (`window.speechSynthesis`) with a pitch/rate tweak per character. It sounds like a
sat-nav. Replacing it with real recordings is the highest-impact audio work available.

**Format to ask for:** mono WAV or MP3, 48 kHz, dry (no reverb — the game adds it), −3 dB peak,
one file per line, trimmed silence. Drop into `src/assets/voice/`.

> You are voicing characters for a fast, cold-weather military shooter called **Ironhowl**. Delivery
> is clipped, professional and confident — radio chatter, not theatre. No shouting except where
> marked. No breathiness, no smirking, no accents played for comedy. Every line is short and lands
> in under two seconds. Record dry and close-mic'd, as if spoken into a helmet mic. Leave 300 ms of
> silence at the head and tail of each take. Give me two takes of each line: one neutral, one with
> slightly more urgency.
